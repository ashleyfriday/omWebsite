Overnight Mountings

Two homepage directions

Homepage A

Homepage A treats Overnight Mountings like a fine-jewelry house. Cormorant serif headlines, champagne-gold accents and warm ivory tones match the luxury look retail jewelers already use with their own clients.

Early on, a scroll-led craft story covers design, casting, delivery and three generations at the bench. Retailer tools, collections and partner quotes come only after that trust has been built.

Homepage B

Homepage B is the modern option. Hanken Grotesk type, bright white space and full-bleed photography keep it current, and image pills inside the headline put product and bench craft first.

It's built for busy store owners, with collections and retailer tools placed early in a clean grid. The Est. 1991 stamp and a trusted-by-jewelers line keep the company's history visible.
